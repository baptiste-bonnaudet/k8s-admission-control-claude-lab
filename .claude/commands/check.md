Ask the user to share their current YAML, or read the file they mention. Then read the exercise README.md to understand the acceptance criteria.

Evaluate their YAML against the requirements. Structure your response as:

**Correct:** what they got right (be specific)
**Issues:** what is wrong or missing (cite exact fields or lines)
**Next step:** one concrete thing to fix

Do not rewrite the YAML for them. Point them at what needs to change and why.
